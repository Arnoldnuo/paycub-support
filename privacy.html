Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

PayCub (薪崽) puts your privacy first.

1. What we collect

PayCub needs no account and does not collect your name, phone number or email. Your work hours, salary, moments and buddy progress are stored locally on this device.

2. Anonymous usage data

If you leave “Anonymous usage data” on in Settings, we record feature usage (for example which screens and features you open, which buddy or theme you choose), app version, OS version, language and whether Plus is active. These events use a random installation identifier and are aggregated. They never include salary, savings, work hours, moment text or your buddy’s name.

3. Crashes and diagnostics

We use Sentry to collect crash stack traces, device model, OS version, app version and session state used to calculate crash-free rates. Crash data never includes salary, schedule, moments or names.

4. Share images

Share cards are generated on your device only when you tap share and are never uploaded to us. You decide who receives the image through the system share sheet.

5. Purchases

Plus subscriptions are billed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. Payment details are handled by the store; we never see your card or billing address. We store only whether the subscription is active, its expiry and the product identifier so we can unlock content.

6. Export and deletion

This version does not export data. You can reset all data in Settings at any time; deleting the app deletes local data permanently. Store subscriptions are managed by your store account and can be cancelled in system subscription settings.

7. Children

PayCub is designed for working adults and is not directed at children under 13.

8. Contact

Privacy questions: hello@paycub.app.
